Landing An Internship

Published on: July 31st, 2025

Blog Author: Dun Toye

Intro With Dun

My name is Abiodun, but a lot of people know me as Dun. I’ve been searching for ways to sharpen my skills and expand my knowledge, basically to become a better version of myself. I’d been looking for the right opportunity to do so, and somehow, it ended up falling right into my lap.

Growth

There were so many ways I approached trying to work on myself. Here's a quick list of things I tried:

  • E-Learning: I didn’t just learn how to code. I also explored Product Management and other valuable online skills.
  • Small Projects: I led a group of over 30 individuals to work on a product concept aimed at solving a real-world problem.
  • Network: I made intentional efforts to expand my circle to people in the same industry as me.
  • Risk Taking: I applied for opportunities even when I wasn't a 100% sure of myself.

When the opportunity for an internship came, through the help of a trusted friend and the little belief I had in myself, I took the chance. That’s how I landed an internship role at a company that was in alignment with my goals.

Believe In Yourself

Risk-taking is an essential step in becoming a better version of yourself. Growth doesn’t happen when you stay comfortable. It happens when you decide to show up for yourself, even when you’re unsure. The truth is, the only person who can truly hold you back is You. Once you start believing in what you have to offer, the doors start opening.
